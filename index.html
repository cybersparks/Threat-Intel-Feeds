About This Data

Breach notifications, ransomware disclosures, and dark web chatter are scattered across dozens of feeds, forums, and social accounts. This page pulls the highest-signal public sources into one continuously updated view, so legal teams assessing exposure, researchers tracking trends, and the public staying informed don't have to hunt for it themselves.

  • Data breaches are sourced from the Have I Been Pwned public breach feed.
  • Ransomware incidents are sourced from the Ransomware.live public incident feed, filtered for ransomware-related reporting.
  • Dark web activity is tracked through curated social accounts covering leaks and emerging threats in real time.

Data is refreshed automatically on a recurring schedule (approximately every 5 minutes) via an automated process. Each entry links back to its original source for verification.
